Privacy Policy

App: Lumeni: Photo Editor
Effective Date: April 6, 2026
Last Updated: April 6, 2026

Overview

Lumeni ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS application Lumeni: Photo Editor ("the App").

The short version: We do not collect, store, or share your personal data or photos. All processing happens entirely on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your photos or any images you edit
  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Crash reports sent to our servers

How the App Works

All features in Lumeni operate 100% on-device:

FeatureHow it works
Relight & 2D Light PadProcessed locally using on-device rendering
AI Depth EstimationUses on-device CoreML models — no data leaves your device
Portrait Retouch & Skin CareProcessed locally using CoreML and image processing
Face Detail EnhancementOn-device face analysis via Apple's Vision framework
Remove BackgroundOn-device segmentation via Apple's Vision framework
LUT Filters & Color AdjustmentsLocal image processing only
Blemish RemovalProcessed locally using OpenCV

Your photos are accessed only while you are actively using the App and are never uploaded to any server.

Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your photo library solely to allow you to select photos for editing and to save edited results back to your library. We use Apple's standard PHPhotoLibrary framework for this purpose.

  • We never read, copy, or transmit your photos.
  • Photo library access is used only for the features you explicitly initiate.
  • You can revoke photo library access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

Third-Party SDKs and Frameworks

The App uses the following third-party technology, all of which operates entirely on-device:

  • Apple CoreML — on-device machine learning inference
  • Apple Vision — on-device computer vision (face detection, segmentation)
  • OpenCV — on-device image processing

None of these frameworks send your data to external servers in the context of our App.

No Advertising or Tracking

We do not use advertising networks, tracking pixels, or analytics SDKs. We do not track your behavior within the App or across other apps and websites.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since we collect no personal data from any users, the App is safe for use by people of all ages.

Data Security

Because all processing is performed locally on your device and no data is transmitted to us, there is no server-side data that could be breached or misused. Your photos remain solely under your control.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
